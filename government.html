Extra UTHS Topics to Master

This section adds the areas that deserve special attention for the UTHS version of the exam.

History

Connect founding ideas to later constitutional change. Know how amendments, court decisions, civil-rights legislation, and political movements changed participation and rights.

Geography

Population shifts affect representation. Reapportionment redistributes House seats among states after the census; redistricting redraws district boundaries; gerrymandering draws boundaries to create political advantage.

Economics

Fiscal policy uses federal taxing and spending. Monetary policy concerns money and credit and is chiefly conducted by the Federal Reserve. Government also protects property rights, enforces contracts, regulates commerce, and provides public goods.

Citizenship & Culture

Separate legal duties from civic responsibilities. Connect voting, jury service, taxes, public participation, civil rights, pluralism, and changing political participation.

Science & Technology

Technology changes campaigns, organizing, fundraising, news distribution, data targeting, public opinion, and the speed at which both reliable information and misinformation spread.

Social Studies Skills

For a source, chart, map, cartoon, or excerpt ask: Who made it? Why? What claim is supported? What evidence matters? Is there bias? What can actually be inferred? Distinguish fact from opinion and correlation from causation.

Short Answer & Teach-It-Back

Prompt 1: Explain the difference between separation of powers and checks and balances. Give one example of each.

Prompt 2: A student says, “The president is the most powerful branch because the president can make any law.” Correct the student using at least two constitutional facts.

Prompt 3: Compare a political party and an interest group. Explain one way each can influence public policy.

Self-check: Did you (1) answer the exact question, (2) name a specific constitutional principle/case/institution, (3) explain why it proves your answer, and (4) avoid vague phrases like “because that's the law”?
