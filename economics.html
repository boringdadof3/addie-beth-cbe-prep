BUILD IN PROGRESS

UTHS exam target

90 multiple-choice questions + 3 essays · 120 points · 3-hour limit.

This hub is being built specifically around the UTHS Economics CBE blueprint: Economics, Personal Financial Literacy, and Social Studies Skills.

Learning modules

1. Economic Foundations

Scarcity, choices, opportunity cost, factors of production, economic systems and incentives.

Trigger: “next-best alternative given up” → opportunity cost.

2. Supply, Demand & Markets

Demand/supply curves, equilibrium, shortages, surpluses, price signals and competition.

Trigger: price below equilibrium → shortage.

3. Business & Market Structures

Competition, monopoly, oligopoly, entrepreneurship, productivity and labor.

4. Money, Banking & the Fed

Functions of money, banks, interest rates, Federal Reserve and monetary policy.

Trigger: money supply / interest rates → monetary policy.

5. Government & the Economy

Taxes, spending, fiscal policy, regulation, public goods, GDP, inflation and unemployment.

Trigger: taxes + government spending → fiscal policy.

6. International Economics

Trade, specialization, comparative advantage, tariffs, exchange rates and globalization.

7. Personal Financial Literacy

Budgeting, saving, credit, interest, loans, insurance, investing, taxes and consumer decisions.

8. Social Studies Skills

Interpret charts, graphs, tables and sources; distinguish fact/opinion; draw evidence-based conclusions.
